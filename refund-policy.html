Cancellation & refund policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

We want every BeWise experience to feel right. Because each consultation reserves Jitendra Khimlani’s time and each remedy blend is prepared personally for you, we follow the policy below. Please read it before you pay.

1. Consultations

  • Reschedule for free up to 24 hours before your session — from My account or by WhatsApp (+91 90542 20952).
  • Within 24 hours of the session you can reschedule once by contacting us.
  • If you miss a session without telling us (no-show), the session is treated as used.
  • If we have to cancel or cannot hold your session (for example the consultant is unavailable or a technical problem on our side), we will offer a new time of your choice at no extra cost.
  • Consultation fees are non-refundable once paid; instead, your payment always remains valid for a rescheduled session.

2. Personalised remedy blends

Your Bach flower blend is prepared for you alone, so it cannot be cancelled or returned once preparation has started.

3. Products

  • You can cancel a product order before it is dispatched by contacting us; the full amount is kept as BeWise credit you can use for any product or consultation.
  • For hygiene reasons opened products cannot be returned.
  • Damaged, leaking, wrong or missing item? Tell us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will send a free replacement.
  • If a parcel is lost in transit or returned to us undelivered, we will re-send it at no cost.

4. Failed or duplicate payments

If money is debited but your booking or order is not confirmed, or you are charged twice, the amount is returned automatically to your original payment method by Razorpay/your bank, usually within 5–7 working days. If it has not arrived, write to us with the payment reference and we will resolve it.

5. Summary

BeWise does not offer cash refunds for completed purchases. You are always protected by free rescheduling, credit for orders cancelled before dispatch, free replacements for damaged or wrong items, and automatic reversal of failed or duplicate payments.
