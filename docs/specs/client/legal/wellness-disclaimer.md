## The full disclaimer

### What Bach flower remedies and aromatherapy are
Bach flower remedies and aromatherapy are **complementary wellness practices**. At BeWise they are used to support
emotional balance and a sense of calm. Jitu Bhaiya (Jitendra Khimlani), Bach Remedy Therapist, chooses the flowers in your
blend based on how you describe your feelings.

### What they are not
- They are **not medical treatment** and are **not a substitute** for diagnosis, treatment or advice from a doctor, psychiatrist, psychologist or other qualified health professional.
- BeWise does **not diagnose** any physical or mental health condition, and we make **no claims to cure, treat or prevent** any illness.
- Experiences differ from person to person; we can't promise any particular result.

### Please keep caring for your health
- **Don't stop or change any medicine** without talking to your doctor.
- If you have a health condition, tell your doctor you are using Bach flower remedies or essential oils.
- If symptoms continue or get worse, please see a doctor.

### Pregnancy, breastfeeding, children and allergies
- If you're **pregnant or breastfeeding**, check with your doctor before using essential oils, and tell us in your intake.
- Remedies for **children** are only given with a parent's or guardian's consent.
- If you have **allergies or sensitive skin**, tell us in your intake. The app will warn you about products that contain something you noted — but please always read the ingredients yourself.

### About the remedy blend
Bach flower remedies are traditionally preserved in a **small amount of brandy (alcohol)**. If you prefer, we prepare your blend
**alcohol-free** — just say so in your intake or tell Jitu Bhaiya. ⚠️ OWNER TO CONFIRM the alcohol-free base used

### About essential oils and aromatherapy products
- For **external use only** unless the label clearly says otherwise. Do not swallow.
- Always dilute as directed and **patch-test** on a small area of skin first.
- Keep away from **eyes**, and out of reach of **children and pets**.
- Stop using a product if irritation occurs.

### Ask BeWise
Ask BeWise is an AI companion. It answers from your own BeWise information and our catalogue, may sometimes be wrong, and
**is not medical advice**. It never changes your remedy — for your blend, please ask Jitu Bhaiya.

### If you are struggling or in crisis
BeWise is not an emergency service. If you are having thoughts of harming yourself, or feel life isn't worth living, please reach out now:

- **Tele-MANAS — 14416** (free, confidential, 24×7, Government of India) ⚠️ verify number before launch
- **Emergency — 112** if you are in immediate danger ⚠️ verify number before launch
- Or go to your nearest hospital, and tell someone you trust.
