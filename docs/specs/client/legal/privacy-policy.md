## 5. AI processing
- If you allow it, we use **Claude (by Anthropic)**, on our servers, to turn your intake answers into a short private summary **for your consultant only**. You never see it in the app, and it never decides your remedy — Jitu Bhaiya does.
- **Ask BeWise**, our companion, uses the same AI to answer questions from *your own* data (appointments, blend, journal feelings, orders) and our product catalogue. It does not diagnose and does not change remedies.
- Your consultant's private notes are **not** sent to AI.
- Our AI provider does not use your data to train its models. ⚠️ OWNER TO CONFIRM against the final API agreement.
- You can turn off the AI summary in IN-11 or Me → Privacy & data, and clear your Ask BeWise history anytime.

## 7. How long we keep it
- Wellbeing records (intake, check-ins, journal, feedback, chats): while your account is active and then **[[RETENTION_YEARS]] years** after your last activity, after which we delete or anonymise them. ⚠️ OWNER TO CONFIRM
- Invoices and payment records: as long as tax law requires (generally 8 years).
- If you delete your account, we delete your wellbeing data within 30 days, keeping only what the law requires us to keep.

## 8. Security
Encryption in transit and at rest, extra protection for case-taking answers, strict role-based access, and an audit log whenever staff open sensitive records. The app only stores **public pages** offline on your device — never your answers, journal, remedy details or payments.

## 9. Children and people you book for
Accounts are for adults (18+). ⚠️ OWNER TO CONFIRM. A parent or guardian may book for a child or someone in their care and gives consent on their behalf; that person's answers are kept as a separate case.

## 10. Your rights (DPDP Act)
You can: **access** a summary of your data · **correct or update** it · **erase** it · **withdraw consent** · **nominate** someone to act for you if you can't · **raise a grievance**.
Most of this is self-service in **Me → Privacy & data** (download my data, delete my account). For anything else, write to our Grievance Officer.
