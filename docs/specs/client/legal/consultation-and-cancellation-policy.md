## Can't make it? Move your session
Sessions are **non-refundable**, but you can move yours to another time in **My appointments → Can't make it?**

| When you reschedule | What happens |
|---|---|
| **24 hours or more** before your session | Free, as many times as you need ⚠️ OWNER TO CONFIRM |
| **Less than 24 hours** before | You can reschedule **once** |
| You don't join or arrive (no-show) | The session is used and can't be moved |

Your intake answers move with your booking, and you can still update them until 2 hours before your session.

## Running late
- Your session ends at its booked time so the next person isn't kept waiting.
- If you haven't joined or arrived **15 minutes** after the start time, it counts as a no-show. ⚠️ OWNER TO CONFIRM
- Having trouble joining online? WhatsApp us at [[SUPPORT_WHATSAPP]] straight away and we'll help.

## If we need to move your session
Sometimes Jitendra Khimlani may need to change a time. We'll tell you as early as we can and offer a new time **at no cost** — it never counts as one of your reschedules.

## Payments that don't go through
A failed payment is **never charged**. If money left your account but the booking wasn't confirmed, your bank or Razorpay reverses it automatically, usually within 5–7 working days. If it hasn't arrived after that, write to us and we'll chase it with you.
