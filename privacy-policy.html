Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

BeWise Holistic Wellness (“BeWise”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through bewiseglobal.in and the BeWise app, why, and your choices. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

1. What we collect

  • Account & contact: name, email, mobile number, city, delivery address.
  • Bookings & orders: sessions booked, products bought, payment status (not your card or UPI details).
  • Wellbeing information you choose to share: answers before a consultation (feelings, sleep, life changes, safety questions), notes for your consultant, mood journal and goals in the app.
  • Technical: basic device and log data needed to keep the service secure.

2. Why we use it

  • To provide consultations, prepare your remedy blend and deliver your orders.
  • To send booking confirmations, reminders, order updates and sign-in codes.
  • To take payments and keep accounts as required by law.
  • To improve our service using anonymous, aggregated statistics.

3. Who can see your wellbeing information

Your consultation answers and session notes are shared only with your consultant, Jitendra Khimlani. They are encrypted on our servers. Our office team sees only what is needed to prepare and deliver your remedy (for example the bottle size and whether you prefer it alcohol-free). If you allow it, a private summary may be prepared with the help of an AI service for your consultant only.

4. Sharing

We never sell your data. We share it only with service providers who help us run BeWise — Razorpay (payments), courier partners (delivery address and phone), our hosting provider Hostinger (servers in a secure data centre), email/WhatsApp messaging providers — and when required by law.

5. Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay’s secure page and are not stored by us. See Razorpay’s privacy policy at razorpay.com.

6. Cookies

We use a single essential cookie to keep you signed in and local storage to remember your bag. We do not use advertising cookies.

7. How long we keep it

We keep account and wellbeing information while your account is active and for up to 3 years after your last visit, and invoices for 8 years as required by tax law. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent (for example to WhatsApp messages or AI summaries), by writing to care@bewiseglobal.in. We will respond within 30 days.
