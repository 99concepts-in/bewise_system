## In short
- Our remedies and aromatherapy products are personal-care items, so we **don't offer refunds or returns** for a change of mind.
- If something arrives **damaged, leaking, wrong or missing**, we'll **replace it at no cost**.

## What we replace
| Problem | What we do |
|---|---|
| Broken or leaking bottle | Send a new one |
| Wrong item or wrong size | Send the right one |
| Item missing from the parcel | Send the missing item |
| Remedy label doesn't match your blend | Prepare and send a fresh blend |
| A product that's faulty when it arrives (e.g. spray doesn't work) | Send a new one |

## How to ask for a replacement
1. Open **My orders**, choose the order, then **Request a replacement**.
2. Select the item(s) and tell us what's wrong.
3. Add **photos** of the item, the packaging and the shipping label. An unboxing video helps us a lot. ⚠️ OWNER TO CONFIRM whether video is required
4. Please report within **[[REPLACEMENT_WINDOW_DAYS]]** of delivery (we suggest 48 hours). ⚠️ OWNER TO CONFIRM

We'll reply within 1–2 working days. Once approved, your replacement is prepared and shipped the same way as a normal order, at no cost to you, and you can track it in My orders. We may ask you to keep the original item, or arrange a pickup, so we can learn what went wrong.

## What isn't covered
- Change of mind, or not liking a fragrance or texture.
- Products that have been opened or used — unless they were faulty when they arrived.
- Reactions or sensitivities to ingredients. Please read the ingredients and the Safety tab before you buy, and patch-test oils. The app warns you if a product contains something you noted an allergy to.
- Damage caused after delivery (dropped, stored in heat or sunlight).
- Reports made after the [[REPLACEMENT_WINDOW_DAYS]] window.
- Parcels returned to us because of an incorrect address — see the [Shipping Policy](shipping-policy.md).

## Consultations
Consultations aren't products and can't be replaced — but you can reschedule. See the [Consultation & Cancellation Policy](consultation-and-cancellation-policy.md).

## Payments that don't go through
A failed payment is never charged. If money left your account but your order wasn't confirmed, it's reversed automatically by your bank or Razorpay, usually within 5–7 working days.
