Shipping & delivery policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Where we deliver

We deliver to addresses across India through trusted courier partners. You can also collect your order from our center in Alkapuri, Vadodara at no cost.

2. Processing time

  • Products: packed and dispatched within 1–3 working days of payment.
  • Personalised remedy blends: prepared within 1–2 working days after your consultation or reorder.

3. Delivery time

Usually 3–7 working days after dispatch depending on your location (metro cities are often faster). Delays caused by couriers, weather or public holidays are outside our control, but we will always help track your parcel.

4. Delivery charges

Delivery within India is currently free. If this changes, the delivery charge will be shown clearly at checkout before you pay.

5. Tracking

Once dispatched you’ll receive the courier name and tracking number by email/WhatsApp and in My account.

6. Center pickup

Choose “Collect from center” at checkout. We’ll tell you when it’s ready. Pickup hours: Monday to Saturday, 10:00 am – 1:00 pm and 4:00 pm – 7:00 pm (IST). Address: 102, Yogikrupa Apartment, 19/A, Nutan Bharat Society, Alkapuri, Vadodara, Gujarat 390007, India.

7. Damaged or missing items

See our Cancellation & Refund Policy — we replace damaged, wrong or missing items free of charge.

8. Services

Consultations are services and are not shipped. Online sessions take place by video call; the joining link is shared after booking.
