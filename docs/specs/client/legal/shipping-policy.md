## Tracking your order
We ship through **Shiprocket** and its courier partners. As soon as your parcel leaves us, you'll get the courier name and tracking number (AWB) by WhatsApp/SMS, and you can follow every step in **My orders → Track order**:
Ordered → Preparing → Shipped → Delivered.

## If delivery doesn't succeed
- The courier will usually try again. Please keep your phone handy and check your address and pincode at checkout.
- If the parcel comes back to us (wrong address, not reachable, refused), we'll contact you to reship. Re-shipping charges may apply. ⚠️ OWNER TO DECIDE (free once / charged)
- If delivery fails because of incorrect details, we reship rather than cancel the order.
